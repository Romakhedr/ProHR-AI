Overview
About Us — ProHR AI
ProHR AI is an experimental smart-recruitment and HR platform that connects job seekers with companies directly, with identity handled through Supabase Auth and Pi Network sign-in. The platform combines an AI assistant with a clean, modern dashboard to explore what a trustworthy, Pi-native hiring experience could look like for small and medium-sized businesses.

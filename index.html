Overview
About Us — ProHR AI
ProHR AI is an independent smart-recruitment and HR platform built on a decentralized verification system that connects job seekers with companies directly, verifying records and skills through blockchain technology. The platform combines the power of AI with the highest digital-safety standards to deliver a seamless, trustworthy hiring experience for small and medium-sized businesses as well as candidates.
